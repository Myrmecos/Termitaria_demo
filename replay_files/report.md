# Definition-Only Glossary and Claim-Level Faithfulness in MedQuAD QA: A Reviewed Experiment Plan

## Abstract

This project asks whether appending a short, definition-only glossary of terms taken from the passage increases claim-level faithfulness in biomedical question answering over MedQuAD, relative to an identical passage-only instruction, where faithfulness means each factual claim in the answer is supported by the passage. Per the project seed, the instrument is fixed: 30 MedQuAD items, Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct, temperature 0, and a ~200-token glossary. The seed also fixes the contribution: confound accounting (glossary content versus prompt length and shape), not the definition-injection question itself. The project deliverable is a reviewed experiment plan, not a measured result; no generations were run and no numbers below are empirical. The team produced four artifacts: a literature gap scan, a falsifiable hypothesis set, an executable protocol, and an adversarial methods review. The review is the decisive output: it blocks execution as written. Arm C (the length/shape filler control) is internally contradictory, because it either uses real headwords and imports priming or uses placeholder headwords and changes gloss syntactic type, so the load-bearing contrast is a two-way contrast rather than a residual. Arm D cannot isolate priming because external definitions can contradict the passage, making priming and contradiction the same variable. The eight-rule decision table, the 10/10 ambiguity split, and the +/-5 percentage-point thresholds are underpowered at n = 30, and no gate tests sensitivity to a known effect. The accepted conclusion is therefore a revision list, not a validated protocol.

## Related work

The gap scan (project artifact `gap_scan.md`) organizes prior work into four clusters, and the framework for the project's contribution rests on two of them. The MedQuAD dataset is the anchoring source: Ben Abacha and Demner-Fushman describe the collection in BMC Bioinformatics, and the repo documents 47,457 question-answer pairs from 12 NIH websites under CC BY 4.0, with answers removed from three MedlinePlus subsets for copyright reasons: https://github.com/abachaa/MedQuAD. Glossary and dictionary injection into prompts is the treatment family. DiPMT injects bilingual dictionary candidate translations for phrase-level machine-translation control (https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07856), terminology-aware MT with constrained decoding targets terminology recall (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.05824), and medical-domain instruction-tuned MT uses `Glossary: term -> term` blocks (https://arxiv.org/html/2408.16440v2). None of these evaluates claim-level support, and the gap scan notes none reports a length-matched placebo; the collector flagged that this claim about their controls is inferred from abstracts and prompt examples rather than full reads, so it needs verification before being asserted.

In-context definition and prompt-shape work supplies the confound literature. ExpertPrompting shows a short fixed role block moves output quality (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14688), and role-design work in in-context learning shows prompt shape matters (https://arxiv.org/html/2509.23501v1). Most important for this project, "Context Length Alone Hurts LLM Performance Despite Perfect Retrieval" shows performance degrading 13.9% to 85% as input grows even with perfect retrieval, and degradation persisting with whitespace padding and with masked irrelevant tokens, at least 7.9% at 30K masked tokens: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05381. A related analysis attributes 14% to 79% of a measured representational shift to prompt length alone (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15990). These are demonstrated in the thousands-of-tokens regime, so for a +200-token manipulation they are an analogy, not a guarantee; the pilot was designed partly to test whether the analogy holds at that scale.

Faithfulness measurement comes from the attribution cluster. FActScore decomposes long-form output into atomic facts and scores support, with the original pipeline reporting that human experts split 18% and merged 34% of generated atomic facts (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251). AttrScore defines attribution error types and its Attribution prompt is the template for the support-labeling step (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06311). RAGTruth supplies a four-way hallucination taxonomy with word and span-level human annotations (https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00396). ALCE supplies NLI-based citation recall using the TRUE entailment model (https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627v2). The gap the scan identifies is the intersection: glossary-injection work is evaluated on terminology compliance in translation, and faithfulness metrics are developed on general-domain retrieval-augmented generation with prompt composition held fixed, so no study crosses a length-, shape-, and priming-matched glossary manipulation with a claim-level support metric on a biomedical grounded set.

## Hypothesis

The hypothesis artifact (`hypotheses.md`) keeps the seed's ostensive question but makes the confounds the discriminating objects. Claim-support rate is defined per item and arm as supported atomic claims over total atomic claims, following FActScore-style decomposition with AttrScore-style binary support labels. Four arms are named: P passage-only, G passage plus a ~200-token passage-derived definition glossary, L passage plus a ~200-token length-matched neutral filler in the same labelled section, and N passage plus ~200-token external-lexicon definitions of the same terms. Five contrasts follow: T1 naive glossary effect, T2 length plus shape, T3 glossary-specific residual over the filler twin, T4 definition source (passage-derived versus external), and T5 definition layer over filler.

The primary hypothesis H1 is directional and mechanistic: appending a passage-derived definition-only glossary increases claim-support rate, and the increase survives a length- and shape-matched filler control, with the diagnostic signature that the reduction in unsupported claims concentrates on claims that mention or restate glossed terms. Two falsification conditions are stated: a non-positive naive contrast, or a positive naive contrast whose load-bearing residual over the filler control is zero or reversed. Four rival hypotheses each predict a different locus: H2 length (the append degrades the model's use of the passage, consistent with https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.05381, with the caveat that the published range is a thousands-of-tokens regime), H3 shape/format (the labelled section does the work, the bundle critique of DiPMT and medical-MT glossary work), H4 priming (naming the passage's salient entities is a salience aid, not definitional knowledge), and H4-prime conflict (external definitions that disagree with the passage induce unsupported claims). A fifth rival H2e keeps "no robust effect" open.

The hypothesis set is explicitly a feasibility instrument. With 30 paired items and one model at temperature 0, the stated escalation threshold is a content effect of roughly 0.10 to 0.20 claim-support-rate points with a bootstrap lower bound above 0.05 and separable confound estimates, and absence of a detectable difference must be reported as inconclusive at pilot precision rather than as evidence of no effect. The contribution framing is a decomposition of any apparent glossary effect into length, shape, priming, and residual definitional components, with the pilot estimating each component's sign and rough magnitude.

## Experiment

The experimenter's protocol (`experiment_plan.md`) is a 12-section executable specification for 30 items times 4 arms, 120 generations, with one ~200-token appended block. Item construction is deterministic: a pinned MedQuAD snapshot with a recorded commit hash, inclusion of collections that retain answer text (the plan names `1_CancerGov_QA`, `2_GARD_QA`, `3_GHR_QA`), a priori exclusion of the three MedlinePlus collections whose answers were removed upstream, five expository question types, a 120 to 400 token passage band, at least 8 candidate glossary terms per passage, one item per focus concept, and seeded stratified sampling with 10 high, 10 low, and 10 mid jargon-density items. The passage is defined as the MedQuAD answer element, and the protocol states openly that this makes the task passage-grounded restatement rather than question answering, since claim support is measured against the passage itself.

Prompt construction enforces mechanical invariance: all four prompts come from one template whose only differing substring is the block slot, with byte-equality assertions on the non-block text, a shared glossary header in all three populated arms so shape is held constant, and cross-arm block length matched within plus or minus 5%. Glossary construction for arm B is copy-exact from definitional sentences in the passage with a definition-only filter and tokenizer-verified targeting of 190 to 210 tokens. Arm C uses placeholder headwords with non-biomedical filler. Arm D uses external lexicon definitions for the same headwords, with a human-coded contradiction tally. Inference is pinned: model revision hash and weight checksums recorded, bf16, batch size 1, arms interleaved per item, and the repo's `generation_config.json` defaults (do_sample true, temperature 0.7, top_p 0.8, top_k 20, repetition_penalty 1.05, verified from https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct/raw/main/generation_config.json) explicitly overridden to temperature 0, do_sample false, repetition penalty 1.0, with a determinism audit.

Measurement follows FActScore atomization (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251), AttrScore-style binary support labeling (https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06311), an NLI cross-check in the ALCE style (https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14627v2), a RAGTruth-style error-type typing of unsupported claims (https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00396), and a 160-claim blind human subsample with reported agreement. Analysis specifies item-bootstrap confidence intervals on median paired differences, supported-claim count as a companion outcome with an attrition test, an eight-rule ordered decision table mapping outcome patterns to hypotheses, confidence-interval-free stratified comparisons, and nine go/no-go gates.

The methods review rejects this as executable. Four blockers were returned. First, arm C is internally contradictory: one section requires identical headwords across arms while another requires placeholder headwords, and no single arm C can simultaneously match headwords, shape, length, and contain zero content, so T3 is a two-way contrast rather than a residual, and R1 (the rule that would certify definitional content) could fire on priming. Second, arm D cannot isolate priming because external definitions sometimes conflict with the passage, making priming and contradiction the same variable, and R1 is gated on a positive T4 that can only arise when D is systematically worse, so the primary hypothesis depends on the confound it claims to control. Third, n = 30 cannot support five contrasts, a 10/10 split, and an eight-rule conjunctive decision table: the review estimates 95% interval half-widths of roughly 4 to 7 percentage points on paired rate differences, which makes the plus or minus 5 point "approximately zero" band unreachable and the +5 point escalation lower bound unlikely to be met even for a true +10 point effect, while the point-estimate comparisons in the length and shape rules will fire on noise. Fourth, the nine gates are all internal-consistency checks and none tests sensitivity to a manipulation of known size, so a protocol that passes can still be insensitive, for example if per-arm support rates cluster near 1.0 or the human subsample is drawn on the judge's own labels.

Should-fix items were also returned: the co-primary framing lacks a rule for which outcome governs when rate and count disagree; the atomizer prompt is self-contradictory and its reliability sample is underpowered; the atomizer's determinism is unaudited and the atomizer and judge share a model family; temperature 0 with a fused-kernel stack does not guarantee bit-identical outputs and the determinism audit is too small; the sampled population is two or three disease collections with five question types, not MedQuAD at large, and the task is restatement rather than question answering; the ambiguity stratum is operationalized as jargon density, which is a different variable; and the item-level correlation test shares an arm's error with the contrast it is asked to support. The review also flags document inconsistency across artifacts on which collections are in scope, and truncation of the protocol text at the point where a second operator would need it.

The accepted conclusion is a revision list required before any execution: reconcile the arm set so each confound has a dedicated matched twin or narrow the claim to one decomposition cell; sense-match or demote arm D and state that priming is not identified; replace the eight-rule table with one pre-registered primary contrast, one estimator, and a reported achieved half-width; add a positive control and a base-rate gate and draw the human subsample independently of the judge; specify a single atomizer prompt and expand the human atomization sample; broaden the determinism audit; and narrow the title and framing to passage-grounded restatement over the sampled collections. The project output is therefore a reviewed plan with documented, unresolved defects, which is what the seed asked for, and no empirical claim about glossary effects.

## Limitations

No data were collected, so nothing here is evidence about glossary effects; every quantity in the protocol is a design parameter. The reviewer's interval arithmetic is an analytical estimate from an assumed per-item claim count and paired-difference spread, not a simulated power analysis, so the realized precision is unknown until a pilot is run. The claim that prior glossary-injection work lacks length-matched controls is inferred from abstracts and prompt examples for at least two papers, per the collector's own caveat. The length confound evidence cited comes from a thousands-of-tokens regime, so its applicability to a +200-token append is analogical. The plan's instruments (claim atomization, LLM judging, NLI cross-check) carry known and unbudgeted error, including the split and merge rates FActScore itself reports. Finally, the reviewed plan is an instrument for a feasibility pilot, not a powered test, and it remains unvalidated because the review's blockers are unresolved.
